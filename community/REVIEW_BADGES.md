# Community Review Badges v5.5

Badges are visible repository signals, not legal certifications or quality guarantees.

- **Rights: declared** — a cartridge includes `RIGHTS.md` with a rights statement.
- **Credits: declared** — a cartridge includes `CREDITS.md`.
- **Claims: bounded** — the cartridge states a claim boundary for fiction, simulation, education, or evidence-linked content.
- **Mobile: pass** — the current PixelForge mobile-readability receipt passes required checks.
- **Runtime: local-first** — metadata/docs declare that the cartridge can run without an account or required remote service.

A cartridge can still need human review even when every deterministic badge passes.
