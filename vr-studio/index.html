WORLD LIGHTING

Shape a world with sunlight, ambient light, color and distance fog. Presets are editable, saved locally in scene JSON, and apply to both primitives and loaded GLBs.

Lighting is basic diffuse shading and exponential distance fog. No shadow maps, volumetric effects or physical color calibration are claimed.

Scene JSON contains transforms and asset hashes only, never purchased models or executable scripts. After a refresh, rebind your local GLB file by the same SHA-256 to display its mesh again.

QUALIFICATION BOUNDARY

Supported static GLB geometry can display embedded base-color PNG/JPEG textures. This is a simple UV-shaded preview, not full glTF/PBR parity. External images, animation, physics, interaction, multiplayer, licensing, headset comfort and frame-rate qualification remain unsupported.
