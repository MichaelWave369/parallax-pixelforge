# Parallax PixelForge v4.0 — Creator OS Alpha

The Creator OS turns PixelForge from a cozy tool-place into a faster world-making experience.
It is designed for creators who have a tiny strange premise and need a guided path into a playable cartridge.

## New Stations

1. **World Seed Forge** — creates editable game-world seeds from premise, biome, motif, and starter scene.
2. **Route Weaver** — stitches scenes into a road-trip / quest route with gates and emotional arcs.
3. **Region Card Rack** — tracks each region as a playable location with readiness, hazards, needs, and NPC hooks.
4. **Questline Composer** — builds small quest chains from steps, rewards, gates, and one-sitting scope.
5. **World Bible** — keeps local canon, sequel hooks, recurring motifs, and claim boundaries visible.

## Boundary

Generated seeds are drafts until accepted by the creator. The World Bible is game canon and symbolic design memory,
not scientific, spiritual, medical, legal, or historical proof.
