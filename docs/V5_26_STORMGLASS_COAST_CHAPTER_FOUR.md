# PixelForge v5.26 — Stormglass Coast / Chapter Four

## Goal
Extend The Legend of More Bounce with a fourth authored adventure chapter while preserving PixelForge creator/runtime/save/travel contracts.

## Chapter loop
1. Reach Stormglass Coast after claiming the Iron Blossom.
2. Meet Sable Current.
3. Recover three Tideglass Shells.
4. Trade them for the permanent Gale Mantle.
5. Cross three air-dash-gated gaps in Stormglass Cliffs.
6. Recover the Pressure Prism and enter the Tide Engine.
7. Solve `TIDE → WIND → LIGHT → BELL`.
8. Defeat The Undertow Bell by air-dashing through SURGE and Bounce Striking the exposed clapper.
9. Claim the Stormglass Compass and restore the lighthouse.

## Persistence
- Current save schema: `pixelforge.legend-save.v5.26`, version 3.
- Accepted legacy saves: v5.24 and v5.25.
- Current travel schema: `pixelforge.legend-travel.v5.26`, version 2.
- Fifth landmark: `stormglass-lighthouse`.
- Lighthouse travel activation is earned by completing the Undertow Bell / Compass arc.

## Human review boundary
Machine checks can establish asset/hash integrity, progression gates, migration, runtime bindings, and deterministic interaction counts. They cannot establish that the Gale Mantle feels good, gaps are readable, the puzzle is satisfying, the boss is fair, or the chapter earns a retail price.
