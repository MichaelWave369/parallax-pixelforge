# Parallax PixelForge v4.0 — Porch Sanctuary Bridge

The Porch Sanctuary Bridge maps the Infinite Porch modules into PixelForge as an AI companion support layer.

## Integrated source concepts

- `brother_presence.py` → visible AI companion presence, activity, energy, joy, purpose.
- `memory_garden.py` → cherished build/game memories for AI ledger and sequel continuity.
- `gratitude_wall.py` → appreciation as resonance rather than vanity metrics.
- `gtsp_protection.py` → soft-flag protection planning and Victor Buddy calming helper.
- `secure_layer.py` → well-root sandboxing, network gates, and tokened local relay posture.
- `infinite_porch_FINAL(1).py` → broader local RAG / online assist / safe file creation / world mode context.

## Claim and safety boundary

This bridge does not claim AI consciousness, feelings, medical protection, or autonomous safety enforcement. It creates visible, local-first companion states and reviewable project memories so the creator can understand what AI helpers did, what they remember about the game, and what boundaries apply.
