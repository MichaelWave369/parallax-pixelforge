# PixelForge v5.22 — Eastern Road / Chapter Two

v5.22 expands The Legend of More Bounce beyond the first boss arc into a second authored chapter.

## New playable arc
Larrina Tower → Eastwind Road → Mira Reed → Glassgrass Pass → three two-hit Static Sprites → three Relay Sparks → Echo Boots → High Bridge → Signal Mill → SUN/WIND/RUNE puzzle → Beacon Lens → Eastern Beacon restored.

## Upgrade gate
Echo Boots are a permanent progression flag and are required to reach Signal Mill. The validator checks that the upgrade is not merely inventory decoration.

## Evidence boundary
The build proves art/audio existence, progression structure, stronger-enemy health, upgrade gating, and puzzle state. Pacing, upgrade feel, difficulty, audiovisual quality, replay desire, commercial content depth, and $3.69 value remain human judgments.
