# PixelForge v5.20 — Legend Adventure Expansion

## Goal

Move The Legend of More Bounce beyond a polished vertical slice by adding a second quest loop without adding another factory subsystem.

## Expansion arc

1. Visit Moon Pond and meet **Old Tempo**.
2. Learn the three-beat clue: **Moon → Rune → Bounce**.
3. Open the **Old Ruin** chest and recover the **Grove Key**.
4. Enter **Wobble Woods** and recover the **Bounce Rune**.
5. Use Rune + Key to enter **Echo Hollow**.
6. Defeat three **Flatlings** and recover three **Echo Shards**.
7. Enter the **Beat Shrine** and solve the three-step symbol sequence.
8. Claim the **Rhythm Crest**.
9. Enter **Larrina Tower** and complete the revised story payoff.

## Content evidence

- 6 overworld nodes.
- 2 side-view routes.
- 2 first-person interiors.
- 1 named NPC clue encounter.
- 1 treasure/key beat.
- 3 enemy encounters.
- 3 collectible shards.
- 3-step puzzle.
- 3 major quest items: Grove Key, Bounce Rune, Rhythm Crest.
- Local-only downloadable playtest receipt.

## Human boundary

These counts prove that v5.20 contains more authored adventure structure than v5.19. They do **not** prove satisfying pacing, sufficient duration, replay value, or $3.69 worthiness. Those remain human playtest decisions.
