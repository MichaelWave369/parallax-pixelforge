# v4.9 Launch Rehearsal + Signoff Gate Alpha

## Purpose

This milestone rehearses the v5.0 private beta launch before any final approval. The goal is to verify the launch packet, tester instructions, rollback path, and go/no-go criteria without turning the candidate into a shipped release by accident.

## New systems

- Launch Rehearsal Desk
- Beta Dress Rehearsal
- Rollback Playbook
- Tester Packet Sampler
- Go / No-Go Dry Run
- v5 Launch Approval Gate
- Private Beta Notice Draft

## Hard boundary

The project remains in hold state until the human release owner explicitly approves v5.0 private beta. No public network, public forum, free-text chat, or unreviewed asset pack sharing is enabled by this milestone.

## Rehearsal rule

Rehearsal artifacts can prepare testers and validate the path. They cannot certify rights clearance, accessibility completion, or v5 shipping status without human signoff.
