# PixelForge v5.23 — The Iron Orchard / Chapter Three

v5.23 expands The Legend of More Bounce with its first reusable hub-and-branch chapter.

## New authored content
- Iron Orchard / Rivet Row top-down hub.
- Tessa Coil required shop progression.
- Bram Gearroot optional Wrench Charm quest.
- Three Gear Apple exploration beats.
- Resonance Bracer permanent combat ability.
- Optional Heart Rivet (+1 heart in the chapter boss).
- Rustroot Cavern side-view dungeon.
- Three armored 3-hit Rustlings.
- Rustbloom Warden boss with Burst → Bounce alternation.
- Iron Blossom chapter reward.
- Six original local-first audio cues.

## Evidence boundary
The machine can prove assets, hashes, gates, encounter counts, progression dependencies, and that the optional quest changes boss health. It cannot prove the hub is enjoyable, the detour is worthwhile, the Bracer feels satisfying, the boss is fair, or the cartridge earns a retail price. Those remain human playtest gates.
