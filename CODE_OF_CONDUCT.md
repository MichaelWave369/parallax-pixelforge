# Code of Conduct

PixelForge community spaces should stay welcoming, creative, and safe for people learning to make small games.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and specific in feedback.
- Credit other creators.
- Respect consent and boundaries.
- Keep public examples family-safe unless clearly labeled otherwise.
- Help new creators ship tiny finished things.

## Not welcome

- Harassment or intimidation.
- Stolen assets or ripped copyrighted material.
- Hidden tracking, manipulative monetization, or deceptive UX.
- Hate content or targeted demeaning content.
- Pressuring people to reveal private information.

Maintainers can remove issues, PRs, comments, cartridges, or assets that break these boundaries.
