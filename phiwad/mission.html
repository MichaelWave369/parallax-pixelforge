ΦWAD Mission Builder

Turn a raycaster map into an author-reviewed Michael & Ori style relay mission. Place two relays, the gold key and an extraction portal, then test reachability before exporting. This does not patch the original game's executable or checkpoint system.

1. Import an authored .phiwad

Maps must fit the current game's 29 × 25 grid; smaller maps are centered on export. Locked doors, secrets and texture assets require a later adapter.

2. Place mission objects

Dark cells are solid. Cyan is walkable. The white marker is player spawn; green/red/yellow/purple markers identify mission targets. Select a tool and click a walkable tile. Coordinate placement also works with keyboard: focus map, use arrows to move the selection, and press Enter to place.

3. Validate and export

Import a .phiwad to begin.

Exports are local-only and preserve the imported package's author-supplied rights declaration. The handoff is review-only until Michael & Ori's own loader accepts it.
